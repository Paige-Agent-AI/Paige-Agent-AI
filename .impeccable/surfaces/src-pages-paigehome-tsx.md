# Public site — surface brief

Scope: the public marketing site (Home, Product, Pricing, Trust & security, About, For agencies,
FAQ, Contact, Resources, 404/error) plus visual consistency on the Solo enrollment path. Mode:
Persuade (Home, Product, Pricing, Agency); Read (Trust, FAQ, About, Resources, legal).

Audience: first-time visitors who run client-service businesses, mostly on a phone. Action: start
the Solo trial ($74.50/mo after 30 days) or log in. Proof: real product surfaces and honest
live-vs-in-build labelling only; no testimonials, no stats. Constraints: login and enrollment
behaviour untouched; motion optional to comprehension; fast on poor signal.

Decisions (owner, 2026-09-28): hero loop uses a message handed to Paige in chat (inbox reading is
in build); team story told as vision with an honest line; funding off the map (payments and
invoicing takes the slot); waitlist + contact write to Paige Operator Workspace forms.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a conductor's score. The Command Mark's slash is the downbeat; every section is
a measure with a beat the reader can follow, and Paige's work lands on the beat. It refuses the
category default — dark SaaS hero, floating UI cards, icon-card grid, testimonial wall.

OWN-WORLD: Obsidian (warm near-black) and Mineral (architectural white) as two real themes; staff
lines — five hairlines — are the grid, the dividers and the capability map; champagne only on the
act (the Send, the primary CTA, the struck/live note). Schibsted Grotesk throughout, display set
heavy with tight negative tracking; the PAIGE wordmark at 0.42em. Depth from layered planes, never
glow or glass. In-build items are present as unlit ghosts, never hidden.

STORY: Paige is your AI chief operating officer; she does the work and you decide what goes out.
The tools aren't the problem, the gaps between them are. Here is exactly what she does today and
what's coming. Start for 30 days.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: header (mark + PAIGE, 4 links, Log in, Hire Paige). Left 6 columns: H1
"Paige is your AI chief operating officer." at ~5.5rem, then "She does the work. You decide what
goes out.", primary CTA "Start your 30-day trial" + "See how she works", price line. Right 6
columns: the hero measure — a four-beat staff with the live loop (message handed in → Paige reads
→ reply writes in your voice → Send / Edit), beat ticks under it, "RUN EVERYTHING." striking with
the mark on beat four. Phone: H1, sub, CTA, then the measure full-width above the fold's end.

FORM: Conductor's score / engraved notation; position 6 of 7 on the ordered list; seed d87a3c65.
Raises: from the cathode gauze — every option present as a ghost, the live one struck forward,
values snap; from the oscilloscope — everything sits on the staff grid; from the papercut — depth
by overlap only, no drop-shadow glow; from the code cascade — one line lit at a time, the rest
recedes; from the letterpress — headlines commit to the full measure.
