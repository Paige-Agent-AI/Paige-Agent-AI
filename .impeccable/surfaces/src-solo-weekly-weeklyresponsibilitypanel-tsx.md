THESIS
An owner can review a weekly responsibility, see its authoritative schedule and last verified check, and open the original work without mistaking a proposal for authorization or a check for task completion.

OWN-WORLD
Extend SOLO's established Obsidian and Mineral workspace: existing --pg-* tokens, compact readable rows, restrained borders, familiar sheets, visible focus and current typography. This is an extension, not a new visual identity.

STORY
Chat prepares and reviews the responsibility. Calendar shows the same process and revision. Verified results lead to existing Operations work. Missing authority, incomplete readback and unknown execution remain explicit; planning creates no schedule.

FIRST-VIEWPORT
The owner sees a concise responsibility summary, schedule with business zone, actual availability and last verified result. Inspection and source navigation precede technical evidence. Empty accounts have one useful planning action.

FORM
Reuse existing conversation header entry, Calendar rail and responsive view-options, and Operations inspector. Long evidence scrolls inside the established sheet. Controls name their action, use accessible labels, and explain current holds. No new dashboard or booking records.

FINISH
unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
