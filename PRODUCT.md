# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Owners of client-based service businesses (coaches, consultants, agencies, thought leaders, advisors) running their business on Paige's Solo workspace, plus the teammates they invite (admins and members). Clients reach the business through a separate, client-scoped portal. Confirmed by the owner 2026-09-28.

## Product Purpose

Paige is the owner's AI chief operating officer: she does the work (drafts, follow-ups, records, scheduling, campaigns) and the owner approves it. Success is the owner running the whole business from one place without learning how to prompt. A non-technical owner must be able to discover and use any feature within five minutes (CLAUDE.md §36).

## Positioning

Agent-driven, not user-driven: Paige surfaces the work, drafts it, and asks for a one-click yes, operating across every department of the business through one governed runtime. Competing client portals are static filing cabinets; Paige reasons, suggests and acts.

## Operating Context

Solo shell (web, React + Vite + Tailwind, Supabase). Owners work in it daily at desk and on phone. Paige is reached through the command spine, contextual invocation and docked panels — never a floating chatbot on authenticated shells.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Every create/update/delete has a callable seam so Paige can do it by voice or text (§10).
- Tenant isolation is absolute; platform (operator) and tenant surfaces never mix (§9).
- Funding/credit content is never a platform default (§2).
- Accessibility: AA contrast in both themes; every animation has a reduced-motion fallback (§11).

## Brand Commitments

Binding (docs/brand/paige-brand-identity.md; owner re-confirmed 2026-09-28 for the facelift):

- The Command Mark (champagne slash + detached orb) and the `PAIGE` wordmark.
- Champagne/gold is spent only on the act — approve, send, confirm, the "on" state — never on decoration.
- Two genuinely different themes, both first-class: Obsidian (dark) and Mineral (light). Light is not "dark with the lights on" (§23).
- The Obsidian / Mineral palette: smoked neutrals, graphite type, champagne light. No generic gradients, no excessive glow.
- Voice: direct, confident. "Paige-run", never "AI-powered"; never "streamline" or "seamless".

## Evidence on Hand

No real customer names may appear in mockups: use clearly labelled design fixtures (brand guardrail). No testimonials, metrics or customer logos exist for use in product surfaces.

## Product Principles

1. Paige drives, the owner approves: surfaces lead with the work Paige has done or needs a yes on.
2. Discoverable in five minutes, no documentation required.
3. One home per capability; extend a surface before adding another.
4. Show what Paige actually does, honestly; never a hoped-for result.
