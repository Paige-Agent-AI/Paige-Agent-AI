## Design brief
Goal: recover the original customer question after an interrupted response.
Human and feel: customer of a coaching/service business; calm, private and in control.
Entry and exit: original CURA transcript/composer; return to that same saved conversation or existing Messages.
System: existing customer shell/components/semantic tokens, compact inline supporting region beside the composer.
Signature: checking the same original conversation instead of sending a duplicate question.
Feedback: immediate live-region status, no decorative motion or haptics; reduced motion is identical.
Rejecting: success inferred from a dropped connection; automatic repeat of an unknown request.
Variants: same customer behavior on desktop/mobile, light/dark; owner Chat remains original.
