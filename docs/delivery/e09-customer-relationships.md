Without that argument, more than one relationship raises42501. client_portal_frame(), brand/config and several existing callers have no selected-business transport. The current UI therefore refuses a multiple-business customer. That is a missing integrated workflow, not completed selection or a frontend label defect.

Small dependency order: (1) read the caller's genuine selectable relationships from original CRM/native identity and brand; (2) bind one explicit selection to original frame, requests and current authorization without changing staff active_tenant_id; (3) implement the existing-shell choice/switch, retiring old reads/private history/drafts safely; (4) prove the joined seven-pillar, wrong-business, revoked, interrupted and returning flow.
