      Keyboard, focus and screen reader
      
      
      Notes
        
        
          
      • [ and ] step frames when focus is not in a text field. \ shows or hides this panel.
        • 
          
      • Tab order in the product: thread rail → New chat → transcript controls (status line, cards) → composer.
        • 
          
      • The status line is one button with aria-expanded; Enter or Space opens “What PAIGE did”. On phone width it opens a sheet; Escape closes it and focus returns to the line.
        • 
          
      • When PAIGE needs an OK or asks a question, focus moves to the card (on a product action or Play). Stepping with the review buttons leaves focus here and notes it.
        • 
          
      • After any product action focus lands somewhere real: Approve → the report heading; Use this, Keep going, Try again → the new answer’s status line; Stop → the footer.
        • 
          
      • Choices are a radio group: arrows move, Space selects, “Use this” submits. Every option is at least 44px tall.
        • 
          
      • Announcements are polite and fire on a change of state only, never per step or per second.
        • 
          
      • Reduced motion: the sweep becomes a solid violet hairline beside the word, the background-work stage dot stops, rows and cards appear by opacity, the Executed mark swaps instantly and fades.
        • 
          
      • Loops (the sweep and the stage dot) pause while scrolled out of view or while the tab is hidden.
        • 
        
      
      
      
    
      

    
      
      
      Proof boundary and surfaces
      
      
      What this prototype cannot prove
        
        
          
      • Real frame order and timing from the server, time to first output, and the 60–90 s research bound.
        • 
          
      • Anything marked C4 or C5: progress while background work runs, completion pickup, asking a question in the main chat, carrying an approval forward in the same answer, and consulting specialists without an approval stop. Those frames are design intent for contracts that do not exist yet.
        • 
          
      • PAIGE’s real wording (the model writes it), real screen-reader behaviour, haptics (annotation only, nothing vibrates), voice, and authenticated runtime on a real workspace (UNVERIFIED).
        • 
        
      
      
      
      
      Surface variants
        
        
          
      • Solo / Agency / Business chat — the full grammar shown here.
        • 
          
      • Studio — same states inside Studio’s own dark chrome; questions with choices already live there.
        • 
          
      • Operator — status line and endings only; no trace (steps are not read there today).
        • 
          
      • Client portal — fast and normal answers, held back, can’t help, stopped, limit. No approvals, background work, research or specialists; no department labels.
        • 
          
      • Live voice — the same states, spoken. Not shown.
