## North Star: one PAIGE AI COO

PAIGE is an AI-native, source-aware, governed business operating system, **not merely a CRM, chatbot, disconnected departments or dashboards**. The owner should be able to ask PAIGE through Chat/voice to understand, prepare, coordinate, execute when authorized, **read back real results**, and continue until the business objective is complete. One Solo-first product supports the owner and scales later to Agency/Enterprise without tenant-specific forks.

Canonical records own business truth; Spine exposes capabilities; Harness bounds work; Orchestration sequences; Trust governs autonomy/approval; Rail receipts evidence real execution; Metrics/Evidence measure; Events/Fabric communicate; Memory/Knowledge/Mind/Agent Intelligence support interpretation and governed improvement. Shared engines are reused by departments and Chat/Live. **No second approval engine, ledger, scheduler, receipt system, router, memory store, metric warehouse, provider gateway or orchestration.**

## Mandatory shell declaration

- Every handoff declares `SHELL: SOLO` or an explicitly authorized alternative; names **exact in-scope and out-of-scope shells**, department and route.
- One **canonical Solo shell** for equivalent workspaces. No tenant-specific route, visual shell, navigation, fixture or account-ID special case. Differences must be server-proven roles, entitlements, data or real connection readiness.
- `PLATFORM_OPERATOR` is a **separate** platform administrative shell and authority boundary. Do not conflate it with Solo. Agency/Enterprise are likewise separately scoped.
- Reuse `src/solo/SoloApp.tsx`, `TenantCommandCenterShell`, canonical route registry, tokens, design system, permission contracts and Solo parity checks.

## Three distinct analytics destinations (owner-locked)

1. **Sales → Performance:** Sales-owned pipeline, opportunities, invoices, receivables, recorded payments, commercial KPIs and approved interactive charts. Auto-render on refresh and update when view/period changes. Do not collapse into Settings Analytics.
2. **Marketing → Analytics:** Marketing-owned campaign, lead/source/UTM, content/channel/acquisition measures and Sales handoff. The current approved Marketing UI baseline is **Overview · Campaigns · Audience · Content · Social · Email · Ads · Lead capture · Analytics**. A later fewer-tabs/Ads/Content concept is **not** an approved material IA change until the owner approves the revised design.
3. **Settings → Analytics:** Distinct general business/operating measurement under Settings **directly beneath Integrations**, before Security & data. Approved internal views: **Overview · Business Health · Operations · Team · AI & Usage · Data Health**. Do not maintain a duplicate top-level Solo Analytics destination; preserve redirect compatibility. Do not duplicate Sales or Marketing KPIs.

Command Center is the cross-functional COO briefing/priority/exceptions surface, not a fourth duplicate analytics department.

It is not permanent permission to ignore later backend instructions.

## Owner priority and agents' execution authority

**Latest explicit owner instruction controls the business objective, scope, UI destination, priority and material design.** Historic roadmap sequence, candidate PR text, coordinator suggestions or hypothetical backend dependencies do not overrule it. Preserve historical decisions, update the current next action, and do not invent new owner instructions.

Within an authorized bounded assignment, the engineering agent owns choices through: inspect current main → implement → test → fix own findings → independent NON-AUTHOR review → exact-head CI → safe merge to live main → authorized deployment → production verification. **No routine coordinator permission loops.** Non-destructive default; no unauthorized customer/provider/money actions or destructive production modifications.

**Fix own-lane material defects. Route and PARK unrelated defects** with concise safe evidence and correct owner; continue safe work. Real source-level file overlap should be negotiated by the actual owners; do not invent department conflicts merely because features share the AI COO vision.

## Operating mandate: PAIGE performs the full COO job

**The user outcome is a BUSINESS OPERATING OUTCOME, not a Chat turn or a feature demonstration.** The COO translates the founder/CEO's strategy into action, builds a resource/capacity-aware execution plan, aligns marketing, sales, client delivery, finance, people and systems, spots cross-domain delivery and financial risk, prioritizes and escalates decisions, manages stakeholders, and closes the loop on commitments. PAIGE must continually understand how work, cash and customer obligations flow across domains.

**COO duty cycle:** understand owner goal/mission and constraints → source-backed department operating snapshot → cross-domain bottlenecks/risks and competing priorities → plan/decision with accountable owner, budget and due date → orchestrated specialist/human work → permission/approval through canonical Trust → execution through existing Spine/Harness/Orchestration → Rail receipts and authoritative external readback → observed business effect, updated plan and next operating review. No one chat answer, static KPI grid or generic automation qualifies as completing this cycle without supporting source and execution evidence.

For service businesses, follow the chain **strategy → Marketing demand → Sales pipeline/terms → delivery commitments/fulfillment and staffing → receivable/collections → customer results/retention → revised business plan**. When PAIGE can only observe or prepare an action, say so. Do not claim that she has performed, followed up or delivered economic results unless proved.

**COO-oriented gap escalation:** If PAIGE cannot perform any step in this cycle, identify the exact gap and its effect on owner decisions: source visibility, canonical goal/target, domain read/snapshot, event/hand-off, actor/permission, budget/approval, actual execution, receipt, follow-up, or business-result attribution. Report whether it BLOCKS THE CURRENT TASK, is FUTURE COO CAPABILITY DEBT, or is a genuine SAFETY HARD STOP. Name evidence state, actual domain owner, smallest valid future handoff and approval requirement. Do NOT silently redesign or build missing backend because a gap was discovered.

This is grounded in the owner's Google Doc `What a COO wants to know immediately` (source → analysis → next actions), `Complete COO Platform Roadmap` (COO brief and operating rhythm), and third-party COO role analysis:

**Each agent must report:** `DEDICATED LANE | COO BUSINESS OUTCOME | UPSTREAM INPUTS | DOWNSTREAM HANDOFFS | OPERATING FABRIC / METRIC VISIBILITY | AUTHORITY / EXECUTION / RECEIPT | PROVEN / MISSING | GAP OWNER & NEXT STEP | UI-FORWARD CURRENT PRIORITY | RELEASE EVIDENCE`.

This is a *contract and gap inventory*, not a fabricated claim that all paths already work, and not an added approval checkpoint. When the owner's current task is UI-FIRST, ship the approved safe UI with honest unsupported states, document downstream COO gaps, park follow-up backend work outside scope and keep safe delivery moving.

## Mandatory new-agent pre-edit declaration

Every new engineering handoff MUST state, before modifying code:

1. **OWNER OBJECTIVE** — exact current requested deliverable, without additions.
2. **CURRENT DELIVERY PRIORITY** — UI FIRST, backend, investigation, acceptance, etc., as explicitly directed.
3. **PRODUCT NORTH STAR** — one governed AI COO.
4. **SHELL / OUT OF SCOPE** — SOLO and other expressly excluded shells.
5. **DOMAIN + EXACT ROUTE** — Sales vs Marketing vs Settings, own files and boundaries.
6. **APPROVED DESIGN** — existing prototype/owner decision, and whether a new material UI decision is truly required.
7. **TRUTH CONTRACT** — existing real data, unsupported capability and honest rendering.
8. **SHARED IMPACT / ACTUAL COLLISIONS** — canonical owners, not conjectured dependencies.
9. **EXECUTION AUTHORITY** — non-destructive through independent review, CI, live-main merge and authorized production verification.
10. **PARKING / HARD STOP** — fix own defects, park unrelated, stop only unsafe affected path.
11. **ACCEPTANCE** — actual owner-visible behavior and evidence, not preview-only or code-only claims.

The coordinator checks **faithfulness to owner intent and inter-agent boundaries**, not the daily engineering design decisions. Engineering agents should proceed independently until completion, escalating only actual exceptional barriers.
