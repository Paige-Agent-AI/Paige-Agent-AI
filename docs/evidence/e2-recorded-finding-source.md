## Source and lifecycle contract

Default callers keep the original result shape and one-round-trip snapshot read. An explicit
valid finding reference may request one original RLS-filtered record with its
original run and registry label. The current snapshot's findings, totals, scan
state and timestamps are not replaced by the selected record.

Actor, tenant, account readiness and reference bind the optional result. Unresolved
or mismatched context, operator scope, malformed references, missing/withheld rows
and contradictory response bindings expose no record. Read failures remain errors,
not "nothing exists". Read cancellation is not cancellation of business work.
Original refresh invalidates the optional read; stale data is hidden while it is
refetched. Original records from unfinished runs may be inspected as recorded
evidence; they never supply completed-check counts or action authority.

The historical read does not call the signal-reference issuer/resolver, loosen its
24-hour/latest/actor restrictions, or call the approval seam. No new query store,
metrics issuer, database function, table, grant or policy is added.
