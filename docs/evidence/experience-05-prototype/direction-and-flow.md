# Weekly Internal Commitment Check — prototype contract

OWNER_INTENT: A SOLO owner delegates one weekly internal commitment check, authorizes its schedule, sees an honest outcome and can control future runs and recall it later.
MUST_NOT_HAPPEN: No live scheduling, external network call, provider action, customer send, domain write, permission change, secondary engine or production UI mounting. Unknown outcomes cannot become a fresh retry.
MUST_PRESERVE: Existing SOLO Mineral/Obsidian palette, conversation context, tenant boundary, owner authority and canonical receipts; future pause/cancel must not claim to terminate an in-flight act.
ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA: Traverse all eight requested steps in the throwaway browser simulation and each modeled failure/control branch. Product acceptance remains UNVERIFIED until the authenticated canonical path passes.

Goal: One owner delegates a recurring internal check of their own Planning commitments and can trust the readback.
Human and feel: Founder-led business owner; calm, accountable, concise.
Entry and exit: PAIGE conversation, with a compact inline detail; exit to the same conversation or Settings reference.
System: Established SOLO, Mineral and Obsidian tokens, compact sans, hairline rows and native semantic controls styled to match.
Signature: The same schedule and run references remain attached when the owner starts a later conversation.
Feedback: One bounded 150ms opacity change on the changing result; reduced motion immediate; no browser haptics.
Rejecting: Metric heroes/card grids and ambiguous success/replay language.
Variants: Owner configures/controls/reads; member denied; a second workspace has independent local state.

First-slice source scope corrected by journey lead: Read only canonical Planning items with status `open`, `in_progress` or `blocked` assigned to OR created by the authorizing owner in the active workspace. Summarize at most 50 items; preserve total and truncation truth. Other owners' commitments, process-health evidence and automation health are outside this check. The deterministic completed fixture has 3 matching items, 3 summarized, limit 50 and no truncation.

Execution permission correction: Authorizing the process schedule does not change per-tool autonomy. The existing internal-read tool defaults to `confirm`; `confirm` and `off` hold future executions. The approved schedule remains durable with a separate intended slot and null actual next run until the owner explicitly opts into automatic internal reads through existing Settings. The prototype shows only a narrow simulated Settings handoff marker, not a second permission dashboard. Happy-path state fixtures explicitly use preapproved `auto`; first-use/default-confirm does not.
