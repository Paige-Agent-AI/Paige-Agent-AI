## User job and state map

The operator's job is to get a document in front of a client for signature and know whether it arrived. Primary action: Send. States touched: success (now names the addresses reached) and refusal (now carries the server's own sentence, distinguishing a non-PDF upload, an unreadable or protected PDF, an empty PDF, a missing uploaded file, and every pre-existing refusal such as a missing workspace contact email or an already-sent agreement). Untouched: first use, loading, cancel, workspace switch, and the draft-only rule, which remains the server's to enforce. Side effect: none added. Scroll owner: unchanged.
