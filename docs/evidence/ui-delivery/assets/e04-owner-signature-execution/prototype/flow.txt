# Original owner signature-request approval and recovery

Goal: approve the exact saved agreement and invitations once, inspect the original result, and return safely after interruption.
Human and feel: a SOLO consultant; deliberate, readable and accountable commercial work.
Entry and exit: existing saved signing review → existing invitation preparation → original document or request history.
System: preserve PAIGE Sales tokens, document-first hierarchy, original form and single parent leave guard; Operate mode.
Signature: the exact document version and every recipient's message remain beside the consequential decision.
Feedback: focus the resulting heading and announce meaningful changes; immediate state updates, no spatial motion or haptics.
Rejecting: optimistic delivery/signature claims, repeat-send recovery, private draft persistence and another approval engine.
Variants: ordinary owner/admin; staff/client denied; desktop/narrow; current authority retired on any business/session switch.
