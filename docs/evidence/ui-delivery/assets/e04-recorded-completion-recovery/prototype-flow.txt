# Existing recipient completion and recovery

Goal: return to the same recorded signature, finish its retained copy if safe, and obtain that original completed copy.
Human and feel: an external consultant client; calm, specific, no pressure to sign again.
Entry and exit: original signing link or interrupted ceremony -> original recorded agreement -> completed-copy download or sender support.
System: preserve AgreementSigning.tsx and agreement-signing.css, existing typography, palette, result panel and token-derived scope.
Signature: distinguish a genuine recorded signature from an unfinished or unreadable completed copy.
Feedback: immediate disabled action and polite status; focus the result heading; no new motion or browser vibration.
Rejecting: optimistic completion, repeated signatures after a lost reply, replacement approval/sender/document stores.
Variants: 1366 desktop /390 narrow, OS light/dark, keyboard/reduced motion; owner/staff remain in original Sales/Portal, not this recipient door.
