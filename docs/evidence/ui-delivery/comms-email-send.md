## User job and state map

The owner asks Paige to email a client. Paige answers with an approval card that shows the email the server stored — To (name and address), From, Subject, and the body clamped at eight lines with Show full email. Approve sends exactly that; Not now declines. Once approved, the send is written as a row in the client's thread before it leaves. That row reads "Sending…" while it is prepared or with the email service, then takes its real status — Sent, Failed, or Not sent (blocked before it left) — or, if the outcome never came back, "Couldn't confirm" with "Couldn't confirm this went out — don't resend." It never carries Approve & send or Edit, and it never counts as a draft. The scroll owner is unchanged: the chat transcript in the PAIGE panel, the thread pane in Conversations; a long email scrolls inside its own bounded region once opened.
