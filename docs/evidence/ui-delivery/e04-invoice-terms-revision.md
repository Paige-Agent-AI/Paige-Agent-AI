## Source-grounded flow and compatibility

An invoice already retains `{id, version}` in its original commercial terms reference. The original native invoice validator compares that version to `tenant_client_agreements.collection_terms_version`; `read_sales_commercial_package` already identifies a changed commercial version. The standing composer previously matched only agreement identity. With a later plan on that same agreement, it could therefore judge an older invoice using dates that did not belong to the invoice's recorded revision.

This repair reuses that exact version contract. A billed invoice linked to the selected agreement must also match its nonnegative safe-integer collection terms revision before the current plan can establish due-to-date, behind-schedule or agreement-progress totals. An unmatched schedule retains its dates and amounts for inspection while its `due` conclusion becomes unknown. The existing attention link opens Sales Terms & Agreements. An unrelated dated invoice retains its independent due-date behavior. An unissued old draft does not invalidate a matched billed invoice.

Canonical invoice totals, outstanding balance, pending request state and distinct provider-live, owner-recorded and test evidence remain unchanged. No source payment record is rewritten. Zero is not substituted for unavailable timing. This is a containment guard, not the remaining complete accepted-versus-working commercial snapshot integration.
