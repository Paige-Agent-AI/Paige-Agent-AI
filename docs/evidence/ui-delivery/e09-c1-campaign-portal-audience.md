# UI delivery evidence: E09 C1 — customers with portal access as a campaign audience

E09 C1 lets a business send an announcement or a promotion to the customers who can open its portal. It uses
the campaign editor the business already has. The visible change sits in one place: the To panel of the
campaign editor (Solo › Marketing › Email › a campaign), and the same `RuleBuilder` in the saved-segment
drawer. That place gains a "Customer portal" group with two choices, "Any contact" and "Customers with
portal access" with a live count. On a promotion or announcement, the To line and the reach line also say
that portal customers who haven't turned on updates and offers are left out. A series does not get the
choice. Nothing new sends, schedules or approves anything. The existing path still does the work: draft,
file for approval, owner approves, worker sends.

## User job and state map

- Purpose: tell the customers who use the portal about something new, without reaching people whose access is paused or ended, or who haven't turned on Updates and offers.
- Audience: Solo owner or admin of the business.
- Primary action: press Customers with portal access in To, then Review and send.
- States: default (Any contact pressed); portal pressed (count beside chip, reach line with Updates exclusion on promotion or announcement); standard or other kinds (chip, no Updates line); series (no group); refused (`rule_invalid` in words); locked campaign (summary "Customers with portal access").
- Exits: Back (save before leave, unchanged), Review and send (unchanged).
- Side effects: autosave writes `p_audience` through `email_campaign_update_draft`.
- Scroll owner: `.campaigns-scroll`, unchanged.
