# UI delivery evidence: EXP03 — Paige's chat schedules a governed email; the approval card says When

Commits 025ff551 (feature) and 828b6a1a (independent review round 1). UI files: `src/components/chat/PaigeConfirmCard.tsx`, `src/components/chat/confirmPreview.ts` (home of the shared `scheduledForLabel`, which now names the time zone), `src/pages/admin/conversations/governedSend.ts` (re-exports it) and `src/pages/admin/conversations/governedReply.ts` (new refusal copy). Backend: the chat's shared email module, the Kit declaration, `paige-turn/resume.ts`, and `comms-email-command` (new `COMMS_EMAIL_ALREADY_SCHEDULED` refusal), declared `Visible-Flow-Impact: yes`.

What a person sees change:

- **PAIGE chat:** asking Paige to email someone at a later time raises the Needs your OK card with a **When** row — **"Sends Tue, Nov 3, 9:00 AM CST"** (the viewer's clock, with its zone, so a daylight-saving change is visible) — and the act reads **"Schedule send"**. An email without a time is unchanged (no When, "Approve"). After approval Paige says it is scheduled and repeats the time the card showed.
- **Asking for the same email again while it is scheduled** (another time, or "send it now") is refused before any card: Paige says it is already scheduled and to cancel it in Conversations first. In the Conversations reply strip the same refusal reads **"This email is already scheduled for <time>. Cancel it first if you want to change it."**
- **Conversations (wording only):** every scheduled time now ends with the zone ("Sends Tue, Nov 3, 9:00 AM CST", "Cancel the email scheduled for …", "Scheduled for …"). See "Stale frames" below.
