The owner wants Paige's "overdue" to mean overdue on their calendar. Starting from not set, they open Setup › Business profile, choose a city, confirm the exact zone and save. The value is read back, and Paige's due and overdue judgements begin. Every other state (admin, refused, failed, unrecognised, unconfirmed) is listed in STATE_COVERAGE with its exit. The scroll owner is unchanged (Settings' own scroll container); the picker is a portal and escapes it.
