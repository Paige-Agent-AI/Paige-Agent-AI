## What changed on the frozen panel, and why (§28 / §13)

The "Paige team context" panel on Settings → Team was approved and frozen on 2026-10-26 while PAIGE could not yet suspend, reinstate, remove, hand over or switch Team conversations, so it listed those as "Coming to Paige, not yet available" and told the person "The actions still coming will follow the same rules. Until then, do them here." This change ships those tools, which would make that copy false. So, and only so:

- the three items move, verbatim, from the "Coming to Paige, not yet available" list into "She can do this for you today";
- the "Coming to Paige" lane, now empty, is not drawn (its heading would otherwise promise nothing);
- the sentence "The actions still coming will follow the same rules. Until then, do them here." becomes "Approvals assigned to someone and a mailbox they connected can't be handed over, by her or anyone, and open work can be handed over only on a standalone business account."

No style, token, class, icon colour or layout rule changed. Before (origin/main 93f567749) and after:

- before: `assets/exp08-settings-team/before-paige-panel-1366-light.png`, `before-paige-panel-1366-dark.png`, `before-paige-panel-900-light.png`, `before-paige-panel-900-dark.png`
- after: `assets/exp08-settings-team/paige-panel-1366-light.png`, `paige-panel-1366-dark.png`, `paige-panel-900-light.png`, `paige-panel-900-dark.png`

Recorded in docs/brain/decision-log.md and in the tier-matrix "Solo Settings → Team" section. The earlier record exp08-settings-team-lifecycle.md describes the panel as it was when approved; this record supersedes that one line.
