## User job and state map

- Writing: a reply being written shows "<AGENT> is writing…" at the end of the stream or thread for everyone who can see it; a private part shows the approved "is answering <Name> privately." line to others instead.
- Addressing: "@" opens one list, in the conversation's box and a thread's — people who can see the conversation, Your Paige team, and in a channel with someone else, Everyone (@channel, @here); a query matches the start of any word, closest first.
- Broadcast: @channel reaches every other member of the channel with Team conversations on; @here those of them who posted there, or whose own read door ran for it, in the last 10 minutes; the author never; never an agent; nobody reached → a plain message. Nobody is alerted (no notification exists yet).
- Failure: a private answer that failed is a neutral box with Ask again; its unknown twin keeps the agent's tint, with Check again.
- Exits: Esc closes the list; Ask again / Check again as in A1b.
