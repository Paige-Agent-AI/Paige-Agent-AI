## User job and state map

PAIGE drafts something a customer will read. The draft's customer-bound fields (a message's subject and body, a booking link's message, what a follow-up action delivers for its kind, a portal recommendation's title and summary) are read with R1's detector against the turn's own vocabulary before anything is filed, carded or sent. A draft that carries internal text is refused, and PAIGE is told what matched and to rewrite it. A card stored before this check existed is read again when it runs; if it carries internal text, nothing is sent or filed, and the owner's card says Didn't run: "Nothing changed. The message included internal system details, so Paige stopped before it went out. Ask her to rewrite it." Ask Paige again asks her for a fresh draft. The scroll owner is unchanged: the Solo transcript.
