## User job and state map

The owner asked for a change and pressed Approve. The card that asked becomes a quiet record of the answer, so nothing is left to press twice. A card on the next turn says Running… at once, then settles to Done, Didn't run, a mixed count, or Couldn't confirm, with one sentence from the server. Didn't run offers Ask Paige again for just those actions; Couldn't confirm says to check first and links to the place to check for CRM actions. The scroll owner is unchanged: the Solo transcript.
