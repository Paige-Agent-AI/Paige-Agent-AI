## 1. Executive Summary & MVP Definition

Paige Agent AI is launching an agent-run operating system for client-based service businesses — an intelligent, two-way client portal that reasons, proposes, and acts on both sides of the relationship: it works for the business owner (pipeline, follow-ups, content, at-risk triage) and for each of their clients (onboarding, answers, nurture, a hyper-personalized portal under the owner's own brand), with one orchestrating brain — Paige — coordinating a standing team of specialist agents (CLAUDE.md §7, §8, §14). At MVP the platform ships for three tenant types at once — **Coach, Consultant, and Digital Marketing Agency** (owner ruling 2026-08-08) — across three subscription tiers (Solo, Agency, Enterprise-as-placeholder), monetized from public launch but gated behind an **invite-only beta first** to catch bugs against a general-public quality bar (owner ruling 2026-08-08).

**MVP scope statement.** The MVP delivers a single-setup, then-autonomous core loop: Paige and her six named VP agents handle client communications (appointment setting, follow-ups, reminders, planning-document creation) over email and SMS, produce initial content (social + ads), coordinate the work across the agentic team, present a Paige-driven customer portal to each tenant's clients (the owner ruling's wording was "AI-driven"; per §3 the platform brands this as Paige-/agent-driven, not "AI-X"), and expose the Marketplace of installable capabilities (owner ruling 2026-08-08). The launch-defining differentiator is the **Owner Trilogy** (Systems Check, Business Vault, Twin Capabilities, Live Competitive Analysis, Newswire, and audit-trail — all pillars at MVP), whose "feeling" must carry through to the tenant's own client portal (owner ruling 2026-08-08; docs/strategy/owner-trilogy-2026-07-26.md). The entire experience is held to a **non-technical UX bar** — a solopreneur through an enterprise operator should feel like they are talking to Paige and managing a team, never operating a developer tool (CLAUDE.md §36; owner ruling 2026-08-08). Super Admin (Paige-on-Paige, Wave S3 operator-scope Trilogy) must be fully functional so the owner runs the platform through the same Paige experience tenants get (owner ruling 2026-08-08).

> **HONEST BUILD CAVEAT (§13, §32).** The word "autonomous" in the core use case is a stated MVP intent, not fully shipped runtime behavior today. The action bus exists as rails (`paige_actions` state machine with `autonomy_lane` = auto/confirm/off) but **has no autonomous worker consuming the queue** — every transition is currently a human-in-chat tool call (docs/PLATFORM-FUNCTION-INVENTORY-2026-07-19.md §3, Ranked priority ②). Building the cron-gated autonomous worker, and giving client-facing Paige a send-free tool surface, are named prerequisites for the "portal that acts" claim. These are MUST-HAVE, launch-blocking work (see §4).

## 2. Target Personas

All three personas launch at MVP (owner ruling 2026-08-08). Paige's platform layer stays coaching-generic and inclusive — the audience is coaches, consultants, agencies, thought leaders, and advisors, and shared copy prefers *practice · business · clients · work · team* over narrow "coaching" language (CLAUDE.md §2). No persona below is served a finance/credit default; funding/credit is only ever a per-tenant opt-in Blueprint a tenant chooses (CLAUDE.md §2; docs/doctrine/paige-practice-blueprints-2026-07-29.md).

### Persona A — The Coach

A solo or small-team coach who sells outcomes and manages a book of clients through onboarding, sessions, follow-ups, and retention.

- **Pain: client comms eat the day.** Reminders, follow-ups, and appointment-setting are manual and get dropped. Paige runs client communications and scheduling from her standing team, giving the coach back their time (CLAUDE.md §3 voice, §7, §8; core use case, owner ruling 2026-08-08). *Grounded capability:* email (Resend), SMS (Twilio, STOP-compliant), and calendar/booking (`paige_bookings`, public `/book/:slug`, Google + Cal.com) are all built (docs/PLATFORM-FUNCTION-INVENTORY-2026-07-19.md §1).
- **Pain: onboarding a new client is repetitive paperwork.** Client signs up, gets a confirmation, signs an agreement — every time. Paige carries the signup + agreement flow: signature capture, PDF generation, and a signed-agreement record already ship (`paige_signed_agreements`, `finalize-agreement` edge function; supabase/functions/finalize-agreement/index.ts).
- **Pain: no time to create content or plan.** Paige drafts initial social + ads content and creates planning documents so the coach reviews finished work rather than facing a blank page (draft-first, CLAUDE.md §36; core use case). *See §4 status note — social channel posting is an ungrounded gap.*
- **Pain: the client experience looks generic.** The coach wants a portal that feels like *their* app, not ours. The tenant's logo and brand override the Paige brand in the client's view (`get_client_portal_brand`, `useClientPortalBrand.ts`; CLAUDE.md §6).

### Persona B — The Consultant

An advisor or independent professional (or a thought leader running a practice) who manages retainer clients, deliverables, and a pipeline of prospects.

- **Pain: pipeline and follow-up discipline.** Deals slip because nobody chases them. CRM/contacts is the platform's most mature domain (`clients`, ~25-panel ContactDetail, `create_contact`/`update_contact`) and sales pipeline is built (`deals`, `pipeline_stages`, stage-based automation via `stage_automation_rules`) (docs/PLATFORM-FUNCTION-INVENTORY-2026-07-19.md §1). Paige surfaces what each client needs and drafts the next move (CLAUDE.md §7).
- **Pain: professional client-facing communication under their own identity.** Consultants need mail that comes from *their* domain, not a shared sender. Per-tenant sending identities (`resolve_tenant_sender`) and email templates ship (docs/architecture/tenant-domain-and-communications-spine.md).
- **Pain: proving value and staying ahead.** The Owner Trilogy's Business Vault (obligation tracking — insurance, LLC filings, domains, renewals) and Owner Analytics + Competitive Intelligence give a consultant an operator's-eye view of their own practice (docs/strategy/owner-trilogy-2026-07-26.md). *Status: Trilogy pillar builds are PLANNED, not yet on main — see §4.*
- **Pain: too technical to bother.** Consultants are not prompt engineers. The non-technical UX mandate means any capability is discoverable within 5 minutes and Paige actively helps set things up rather than only diagnosing gaps (CLAUDE.md §36; owner ruling 2026-08-08).

### Persona C — The Digital Marketing Agency

A parent tenant running a book of business — multiple sub-accounts (their own clients/brands) under one roof.

- **Pain: managing many client accounts at once.** The Agency account is the platform's compounding unit; it owns sub-accounts with structural isolation — a sub-account is never an agency, enforced at the database layer (CLAUDE.md §51 ABSOLUTE INVARIANT; §17 growth atom). Sub-account provisioning ships (`create_subaccount`, `switch_into_subaccount`, `list_subaccounts` MCP tools).
- **Pain: producing content and campaigns at volume.** Agencies live on landing pages, funnels, forms, and creative. Vibe Studio ships one conversational entry that classifies a brief and drafts pages/forms/funnels/images (docs/PLATFORM-FUNCTION-INVENTORY-2026-07-19.md §2). *Gap: funnels are v1, the in-session campaign engine is a stub, and the visual-critique loop is code-complete but gated off in prod.*
- **Pain: brand consistency across every client's portal.** An agency needs each client's portal to carry that client's brand. Brand cascades to sub-accounts via `resolve_tenant_brand`'s recursive parent walk, so a sub-account inherits the agency brand (docs/doctrine/tier-matrix.md).
- **Pain: power-user reach without technical overhead.** Agencies may be more savvy, but the baseline UX stays non-technical; automations are wired through the n8n bridge behind the scenes (`paige_workflow_registry`; owner ruling 2026-08-08; CLAUDE.md §36).

> **OWNER INPUT REQUIRED** — Whether any of the three personas' MVP journeys assume **native social-channel connection/posting** or **native Zapier**. Neither is grounded as built: social connectors/posting appear in no inventory, callable-seams, or architecture doc; Zapier appears only as an external MMA-ecosystem rail, while **n8n** is the grounded native automation bridge (docs/architecture/ECOSYSTEM_DATA_OWNERSHIP_MAP.md). Confirm scope before a persona story promises either.

## 3. Core End-to-End Use Case

The stories below express the owner-ruled core loop (owner ruling 2026-08-08). Each is written as the persona would experience it. Acceptance criteria are gated by §13 honesty: where a capability is rails-only or unbuilt today, the criterion states the required end-state and is cross-referenced to the §4 build status.

### Story 3.1 — Single setup, then it runs

**As a** coach/consultant/agency owner, **I want** to complete one guided setup and then have Paige run the operation, **so that** I stop hand-running my client operations.

Acceptance criteria:
- On first login, Paige runs a **Systems Check** that surfaces setup gaps **one at a time, prioritized by importance, and actively helps configure each** (not just instructions) — comms senders, website, social accounts, automation wiring, company info, CRM contacts, pipeline, revenue tracking, payment processors, and payment methods (owner-locked Systems Check MVP, owner ruling 2026-08-08).
- Systems Check runs at agency, solo, and sub-account levels (owner ruling 2026-08-08; §51 per-tier requirement).
- Setup writes through callable seams (RPC/edge/config-as-data) so Paige can drive it from chat, not only a human clicking (CLAUDE.md §10).
- *Status honesty:* Systems Check is defined as a 30-check catalog (docs/strategy/owner-trilogy-2026-07-26.md) but is **PLANNED (#80), not yet built on main** (docs/doctrine/canonical-build-order.md). MUST-HAVE, launch-blocking.

### Story 3.2 — Autonomous client communications

**As a** tenant owner, **I want** Paige to set appointments, send follow-ups and reminders, and create planning documents without me driving each step, **so that** clients are cared for while I focus on delivery.

Acceptance criteria:
- Paige books/reschedules against the tenant's calendar (`paige_bookings`, Google + Cal.com — **built**, docs/PLATFORM-FUNCTION-INVENTORY-2026-07-19.md §1).
- Follow-ups and reminders fire on schedule via stage-based automation (`stage_automation_rules` — **built**).
- Actions honor the autonomy tier per action kind: 🟢 auto performed-and-logged, 🟡 draft-to-approval, 🔴 human-briefed (CLAUDE.md §16; `autonomy_lane` enum).
- Every autonomous action is reported honestly — a queued or drafted action is never reported as delivered (CLAUDE.md §13).
- *Status honesty:* true autonomy requires the **cron-gated action-bus worker**, which is **not built** (docs/PLATFORM-FUNCTION-INVENTORY-2026-07-19.md §3, Ranked priority ②). MUST-HAVE, launch-blocking.

### Story 3.3 — Email and SMS

**As a** tenant owner, **I want** Paige to communicate with clients over email and SMS under my identity, **so that** outreach looks like it comes from my business.

Acceptance criteria:
- Email sends via the tenant's own sending identity/domain (`resolve_tenant_sender`, `send_composed_email`, email templates — **built**).
- SMS sends via Twilio with STOP compliance (`send_sms` — **built**), *with the honest limit that only one shared Twilio number + one global A2P flag exist today; per-tenant telephony and phone/calling are unbuilt/deferred* (docs/PLATFORM-FUNCTION-INVENTORY-2026-07-19.md §1; docs/architecture/platform-operator-tenant-200.md).
- Message content passes the §3 voice gate at the model-router boundary (banned: "AI-powered", "seamless", "streamline", "empower"; docs/PAIGE-CALLABLE-SEAMS.md).

### Story 3.4 — Initial content creation (social + ads)

**As a** tenant owner, **I want** Paige to produce initial social posts and ad creative I can review and approve, **so that** I show up consistently without staffing a content team.

Acceptance criteria:
- Paige drafts copy and generates images through the model-router seams (`generate-image`, `callModel`; Vibe Studio generation routes — **built** for pages/forms/funnels/images, docs/PLATFORM-FUNCTION-INVENTORY-2026-07-19.md §2).
- Drafts are presented draft-first for one-click approval (CLAUDE.md §36).
- Content stays coaching-generic unless the tenant opted into a vertical Blueprint (CLAUDE.md §2).

> **OWNER INPUT REQUIRED** — **Social-channel connection and posting/scheduling is not grounded as built** in any inventory, seams, or architecture doc. "Initial content creation (social)" can be delivered today as *drafted creative/copy* handed to the tenant, but **publishing to social channels has no shipped connector**. Confirm whether MVP scope is draft-only or includes native posting (which would be net-new build).

### Story 3.5 — The agentic team coordinating autonomously

**As a** tenant owner, **I want** Paige to run a team of specialist agents that coordinate with each other, **so that** I am managing a team, not operating a tool.

Acceptance criteria:
- Paige (orchestrator) plus **six VPs — VERA, NEXUS, CURA, MENTOR, MERIT, ZION** are present and attributed in the product (docs/doctrine/paige-c-suite-roster.md; shipped VP surfacing #243–#247 on main). Dynamic specialist spin-up per CLAUDE.md §14.
- Cross-team coordination follows the router pattern: classify task → route to the owning department/VP → apply autonomy tier → execute/draft/brief (CLAUDE.md §16).
- Team dispatch and status are a **chat act, streamed in the transcript** — never a separate "agent manager" tab (CLAUDE.md §20).
- Agents are distributed across UI surfaces (not bunched in one chat), with Paige reachable from chat to all of them (owner ruling 2026-08-08).

> **OWNER INPUT REQUIRED (a)** — **Agent placement across UI surfaces:** which surface hosts each of the six VPs. Owner-owed; the crew must not invent it.
> **VP remit conflict (must resolve):** the shipped code (`PaigeAttribution.tsx` VP_ROSTER, `vpDepartments.ts`) and the roster doctrine doc assign *different* remits to VERA/NEXUS/CURA/MENTOR/MERIT/ZION. Code is the current shipped source of truth. Any BRD line naming a VP's responsibility must resolve which is authoritative — **OWNER INPUT REQUIRED**.

### Story 3.7 — Marketplace

**As a** tenant owner, **I want** to browse and install capabilities from a Marketplace, **so that** I extend my Paige without a developer.

Acceptance criteria:
- Marketplace is a first-class OS surface (CLAUDE.md §35), tenant-scoped via `marketplace_catalog_for_tenant`, with browse/install/uninstall/recommend seams (`marketplace_*` MCP tools; docs/architecture/MARKETPLACE-DATA-MODEL.md).
- Visibility cascades by tier (Enterprise ≥ Agency ≥ Solo; fail-closed to Solo) (src/lib/__tests__/marketplaceTierSubstrate.test.ts). *Code-lag note (§13): the cascade element is stored today as `Academy` in code (`available_to_tiers`, `current_tenant_tier()`, `_mp_tier_cascade_keys()`); the taxonomy rename to `Agency` is an owed follow-up — see §5.*
- Slices 0–2 (tier/role/publish substrate; agency-curation allowlist; tenant-side curation visibility) are **shipped to main** (#277, PRs #382/#391/#392). **Persona L1** (voice-first default prompt) is **shipped** (#384).
- **Per-sub-account curation is MVP scope (owner ruling 2026-08-08).** Today Slice 1 curation is all-or-nothing — an item is either "available to sub-accounts" or not. MVP requires the agency to pick **WHICH sub-accounts get WHICH marketplace items** (per-sub-account targeting, not a single agency-wide allowlist). This ships as **its own PR after the tier-rename reversal**, but it **is** MVP scope, not post-launch (§4 MUST-HAVE). Tier posture (§51): the agency curates only within its own book of sub-accounts; a sub-account never sees another sub-account's or the parent's private curation.

> **OWNER INPUT REQUIRED (c)** — the **specific Marketplace items available at launch**. The substrate ships, but no doc enumerates the launch catalog. Also confirm whether a Marketplace "Slice 3" is scoped (Slice-3 commits found belong to Practice Blueprints, not #277).

### Story 3.8 — Paige AI Chat: durable memory + real tasking (MVP)

**As a** tenant owner (or their client), **I want** my conversations with Paige to persist and stay coherent over time, and for the things I ask her to track to become real tasks, **so that** Paige feels like a continuous team member, not a chat that forgets.

Three owner-ruled MVP requirements (owner ruling 2026-08-08; §7 intelligent portal, §9 tenant isolation, §13 honest, §46). *Verified current state on prod before asserting (§13):* both `paige_conversations` and `paige_chat_threads` **exist**.

Acceptance criteria:
- **(a) Conversation COMPACTION.** When a thread grows past the model context/cost window, Paige compacts it into an **intelligent summary that preserves the relevant context** (not a hard truncation) — the same discipline as Claude Code's session-summary hook — and **BOTH the compaction and the full transcript persist**. *Status (§13/§32):* a **rolling-summary watermark** (`paige_chat_threads.summary_through_seq`) + a session-summarizer prompt exist in `paige-ai-chat` over a most-recent-50-turn window; but the summarizer prompt is currently **credit/funding-flavored** (a §2 flag — must be made coaching-generic) and the "relevance-preserving + both-persist" bar is **not verified end-to-end**. MVP work: generalize + §2-clean the summarizer, and prove both artifacts persist.
- **(b) PERSISTENT conversation history.** History is **durable and keyed to user + tenant, never to a session id** — the user returns to a prior thread and continues it. *Status:* multi-chat persistence **EXISTS** for the "Your Paige" owner workspace (`paige_chat_threads` + `paige_chat_turns`, RLS + create/append RPCs, owner-list index, sliding retention window; migrations `20260711201150`, tenant isolation `20260713024606`). MVP work: verify user+tenant keying (not session) across **all** chat surfaces including the client portal (§9/§51).
- **(c) PERSISTENT TASKING.** When the user asks Paige to track something, she creates a **REAL durable task in the tenant task system**, **auto-linked back to the source conversation** — not a chat mention that evaporates. *Status (§13):* **not confirmed wired** this pass — needs a durable task row + a conversation→task link, verified end-to-end (§32). MVP build.

> **HONEST NOTE (§13).** (a) and (b) have real substrate on prod today (summary watermark, persistent threads); (c) is unverified and likely net-new. All three are MUST-HAVE MVP (see §4). §46 is cited by the owner ruling as the governing chat/memory doctrine; the crew did **not** locate a numbered §46 in `CLAUDE.md` this pass — **OWNER-OWED** doctrine-file reconciliation (the ruling stands; the §-paste is owed).

## 6. Systems Check MVP

### 6.1 Behavior model

The Systems Check is the first Owner-Trilogy pillar and the launch face of the platform's differentiation (owner ruling 2026-08-08; Pillar 1 of the Owner Trilogy, `docs/strategy/owner-trilogy-2026-07-26.md`). Paige inspects the tenant's actual setup state, then works the gaps rather than merely listing them. Three behaviors are locked:

- **One at a time, not a wall.** Paige surfaces the single highest-priority missing piece, resolves it with the tenant, and only then moves to the next. This is the §36 draft-first / proactive-surfacing pattern — the tenant opens a queue Paige has already reasoned over, not a settings maze they must learn to navigate (CLAUDE.md §36).
- **Priority-ordered by importance.** Gaps are ranked so the tenant fixes what unblocks the core end-to-end use case first (comms before, say, payment-method labels) (owner ruling 2026-08-08).
- **Hands-on assist, not instructions.** Paige does not hand over a checklist and walk away. She actively helps set the missing piece up — connecting a sender, drafting company info, proposing a website in Vibe Studio — consistent with the §14 "Paige never works solo, she does the work through her team" mandate and the §36 "diagnose AND help set up" constraint (owner ruling 2026-08-08; CLAUDE.md §14, §36).

**Runs at all three operating tiers.** The Systems Check fires at **agency**, **solo (standalone tenant)**, and **sub-account** levels, each scoped to its own book per the §51 tier matrix and the ABSOLUTE INVARIANT (a sub-account is never an agency; it checks its own setup, never the parent's) (owner ruling 2026-08-08; CLAUDE.md §51 + `docs/doctrine/tier-matrix.md`).

**Scope note (§13 honesty).** The strategy source defines the Systems Check MVP as a **30-check catalog** (`docs/strategy/owner-trilogy-2026-07-26.md` L226); the ten checks the owner locked for this BRD (below) are the launch-blocking core within that catalog. ## 8. Non-Technical UX Principles (design constraints)

The whole platform must feel non-technical — from solopreneur to enterprise, nobody should have to *think* to use it (owner ruling 2026-08-08). This is the client-facing complement to §36 (intuitiveness is the moat) and binds every surface as a hard design constraint, testable by the adversarial verifier and design critic (§11, §25, §36).

**Testable constraints:**

1. **Feels like talking to Claude / ChatGPT.** The primary interaction is conversational, not a form maze or a prompt window the user must learn to drive. Paige is an agent-driven surface, not a user-driven prompt window (CLAUDE.md §36; owner ruling 2026-08-08). **Test:** a first-time tenant accomplishes a real task by describing it in plain language, with no field-by-field configuration required.
2. **Diagnose AND set up — never just diagnose.** Paige and her team do not stop at surfacing a gap; they actively help set it up (hands-on), per §14 (Paige does the work through her team) and the Systems Check assist behavior in §6 (owner ruling 2026-08-08). **Test:** every diagnosed gap ends in Paige offering to perform or draft the fix, not in an instruction the user must execute alone.
3. **5-minute discoverability (§36).** A non-technical coach with zero prompt-engineering skill must discover and use any capability within their first five minutes, without documentation or a tutorial (CLAUDE.md §36). **Test:** the design-critic/verifier runs this exact question on every user-facing surface; failing it blocks ship.
4. **Draft-first, one-click approval, proactive surfacing, team framing.** Use at least one (ideally several) of the five §36 mechanical patterns on every user-facing build: proactive surfacing (Paige tells the user what needs doing), draft-first (Paige produces finished work, human approves), domain-expert framing (Paige speaks the tenant's business language, never "let me query the database"), one-click approval (Approve is a button, not a reformulated prompt), and §14 team framing (the user manages a team, not drives a tool) (CLAUDE.md §36).
5. **Agencies may be more power-user; baseline stays non-technical.** Agencies can be more savvy and get denser, more powerful affordances, but the baseline UX contract stays non-technical across every tier — the power-user path is additive, never a prerequisite (owner ruling 2026-08-08). **Test:** no capability is reachable *only* through a technical/power-user path; there is always a conversational, non-technical route.
6. **No dev-tool language leaks (§36, §3).** Never surface backend table/function names, "invoke the tool," "run this skill," or agent jargon in tenant- or client-facing copy (CLAUDE.md §11 "no amateur tells," §36 domain-expert framing).
