## 2. One SaaS, not a collection of features

- Work unit = an end-to-end buyer/operator job with actual authenticated UI, domain/provider outcomes, durable receipts, reload/return, explicit approvals and safe error recovery; PRs, capability counts, pages and synthetic fixtures are only incremental engineering evidence.
- SOLO is one shared current/future-tenant implementation. Equivalent plan/member/role/connection/provider/consent conditions yield equivalent behavior. Never bind a product feature to Antonio/Academy or a QA tenant.
- Platform Operator governs PAIGE's own relationships, subscriptions, billing and revenue (L1). A SOLO customer's own client sales and invoices (L2) remain that tenant's. L3 usage/overages and L4 marketplace are separate if genuinely implemented. $0 free trial is not $74.50 collected revenue; providerless promotional accounts are not paying subscribers.
- EXP00 completes discovery/signup/email/Stripe verified trial/workspace → **REUSE** existing EXP01 Meet PAIGE → first approved act/Rail → **Operator** commercial/customer success experience. EXP01 owns its own internal implementation. E09 Customer Portal is tenant-owned client view, not a fifth SaaS account shell.
- Owner Oct15 prelaunch: autonomous proportionate engineering tests + documented independent review + required exact-head CI → safe merge/deploy and web/Edge/database readback → owner tests the entire integrated experience once. Real payments, cancellation, destructive changes, privilege elevation and unauthorized external sends remain owner-held.
